# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile application that presents this text handles personal data. The App is a single-player word-chain puzzle. It can be played in Turkish and English. It does not ask you to create an account. It does not show ads, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

This text is written for Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”), and the transparency duties that come with the third-party services the App uses.

## 1. Data controller
Data controller: Murat Can
Email: muratcanappsupport@gmail.com

This text applies to the mobile application that presents it. Send KVKK and other privacy requests to the email address above.

## 2. Data we do not collect The App does not collect or ask for:

- An account based on your name, email address, phone number, or password
- Payment card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. Apple processes the payment
- Location
- Contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera images
- An advertising identifier (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data
- Chat, a friend list, or a public profile that you write
The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Tracking is off in the privacy manifest.

## 3. Data that stays on your device
Game progress is not written to our server. The following stays in local storage on your device:

- Completed and unlocked levels, best score, stars, and attempt counts
- All-time, weekly, and monthly score totals
- Coins, hints, experience, rank, streak, daily quests, chests, achievements, and cosmetic choices
- The daily-chain result and the rush-mode best score
- App language, game language, sound, haptics, and the notification preference
- Whether onboarding has been seen
- A random install identifier generated for analytics, and the first-open date
- A cache of level lists and version-check settings downloaded earlier
- Transaction identifiers stored so the same purchase does not grant coins twice on that install

Deleting the App deletes this local data. Restoring a device backup may bring it back. Coins are a consumable virtual item. Deleting the App deletes the local balance, and Apple’s restore-purchases flow does not return spent coins.

Event parameters can include gameplay detail such as level number, score, stars, combo, duration, wrong-letter count, hint use, and coin balance. Puzzle answers, your name, your email, and your Game Center display name are not written into Analytics events. This data is not kept in order to identify you. The privacy manifest marks product interaction and other usage data as not linked to identity and not used for tracking. The exception is that, after you sign in to Game Center, RevenueCat can attach the purchase record to Apple’s player identifier. That identifier is not your name. Your Game Center display name stays with Apple.

## 5. Third parties
We do not sell your data. We do not give it to an ad network. These processors receive data only as needed to provide the service:

| Processor | What it receives | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Analytics | Events, install identifier, summary properties, device and app diagnostics | United States and other countries where Google processes data |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Crashes, errors, and diagnostics | United States and other countries where Google processes data |
| Google Cloud Firestore | Nothing is written about you. The App reads level and settings documents | United States and other countries where Google processes data |
| Apple | Game Center profile and scores, App Store payment, local-notification permission, the device operating system | As set by Apple’s countries and terms |
| RevenueCat | Purchase record and the player or anonymous app identifier | United States |

Firestore security rules should keep client writes closed so a modified app cannot change levels or settings. Your progress is not sent to that database.
When you share a daily chain, the text goes to the app you pick on the iOS share sheet. That app’s own terms apply.

## 6. International transfers
Provider servers may be outside Türkiye, in particular in the United States. Under KVKK article 9, the transfer is necessary to provide the service. Google’s, Apple’s, and RevenueCat’s own data-processing terms and standard contractual arrangements apply to that transfer. We do not send this data to build an advertising profile or to sell it to a recipient abroad.

## 7. Retention

- Game data on the device remains until you delete the App or clear the device data.
- Crashlytics records follow Google’s product period. Crash records in the product are generally kept for up to 90 days.
- User-level Analytics events are kept for the period selected in the Firebase console. The default is 2 months and it can be extended to 14 months. Aggregate reports may remain longer.
- RevenueCat keeps the transaction record for as long as needed to deliver the purchase, prevent a duplicate grant, and review fraud.
- Game Center scores and the display name remain under Apple’s Game Center terms. To remove them, use the Game Center tools on your Apple account.

When a period ends, the data is deleted, anonymized, or dropped from the processor’s system.

## 8. Notifications
Notifications are local. The App does not send remote push notifications. If you allow them, the device schedules reminders such as the daily hint, streak, come-back, and daily chain. You can withdraw permission in Settings. The in-app notification switch also cancels scheduled local notifications.

## 9. Purchases
Coin packs are consumable. The charge is billed to your Apple ID. We never receive your card details. The purchase is visible to RevenueCat as a product identifier and a transaction result, so coins are granted once.
Refunds follow Apple’s refund rules. You request a refund from Apple. Consumed virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be transferred or sent to another player.

## 10. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Apple Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13. If you believe such data has reached us, write to muratcanappsupport@gmail.com. We will delete the record we can access.
Placing the App in the Kids category would require a separate review, because third-party analytics and crash tools are not appropriate there. This policy describes the current general-audience App.

## 11. Security
Local records sit in the operating system’s app container. Network calls use the encrypted connections of Firebase, Apple, and RevenueCat. No method is absolutely secure. Keeping your device locked and updated is your responsibility.

## 12. Your rights
Under KVKK article 11, you may ask the controller to:

- Learn whether your personal data is processed
- Request information if it has been processed
- Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose
- Know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad
- Ask for correction if it is incomplete or wrong
- Ask for deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK article 7
- Ask that correction and deletion be notified to the third parties who received the data
- Object to a result against you produced solely by automated analysis
- Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing

Send the request to muratcanappsupport@gmail.com with enough information to verify your identity. We reply within 30 days, depending on the nature of the request. If you believe the processing violates KVKK, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.

If you live in the European Economic Area, you also have, where they apply, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. You may contact your supervisory authority. Because the App does not keep a user account, many requests end with deleting the data on the device and using Apple’s and Google’s own account tools. The Analytics install identifier is not matched to your name, so separating one person from the events is not always possible. If you send the install identifier you have, or an approximate date and device, we will try to match it.

Game Center data is requested from Apple. Payment records are requested from Apple. RevenueCat records are requested from us. Deleting the App deletes local progress. Unlinked events in Firebase drop when their retention period ends.

Continuing to use the App after the change means you accept the current text. If a new process needs fresh consent, we will ask for it separately.
